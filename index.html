Ryotaro Niwa

I am a PhD student at the University of Tokyo supervised by Mio Murao. Starting in September 2026, I will be a PhD student at Caltech.

My research focuses on quantum information theory: I study the fundamental limits that physics places on information processing and aim to understand physics from an information theoretical perspective. I am also interested in designing algorithms that benefit from the unique power of quantum mechanics.

My recent work is especially about transformation/learning of unknown quantum channels, using tools such as the representation theory of the symmetric group, semi-definite programming (SDP), and quantum singular value transformation (QSVT).
